Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short

Digital Plant does not collect, share or sell any data about you. The game works offline: it has no servers, accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

What is stored on your device

Only what the game needs: your progress in three save slots and your settings (language, volume, vibration). These files live in the app's sandbox on your iPhone. We never see or receive them.

What the game does not do

  • It does not go online or contact any server.
  • It does not require sign-up and creates no accounts.
  • It uses no advertising or analytics services and no third-party libraries that collect data.
  • It does not ask for access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or any other device data.
  • It has no in-app purchases.

Backups

If iCloud Backup is on, or you back up your iPhone to a computer, the game's files may be included with the rest of your app data. That happens at the iOS level under Apple's rules; we have no access to such backups.

Children

Because the game collects no data, it collects no data from children either. The game has an age rating of 4+ on the App Store.

This website

The site is static: no cookies, counters or external scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; as a hosting provider, GitHub may keep its own request logs under its own rules.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. The game will keep working offline either way.
