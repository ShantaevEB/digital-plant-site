Support

Answers to common questions. If yours is not here, email us.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. The game has three save slots, each with its own plant: Continue opens the latest one, Load opens any of them. It saves by itself every 30 seconds and whenever you leave the app or close it. No network or account is needed.

How does income work while I am away?

When you come back, the game works out what the shop did without you and shows a summary, “You were away”: how much was produced and shipped on contracts, and whether there was downtime. At most 24 hours of absence are counted; a longer break is not simulated. Machines break while you are gone and wait for repair, which is why technologies that repair and launch orders by themselves pay off.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings: from the title screen or from the pause menu (the pause button at the top). Choose Русский, English or Español. On first launch the game follows your iPhone's language: Russian, Spanish, or English for everything else.

There is no sound, or it is too loud.

Check the side switch and the volume on your iPhone: the game follows them. Settings has separate sliders for music, effects, shop noise and interface, and a switch for vibration.

How do I replay the tutorial?

The “?” button at the top of the screen opens How to play. Settings has a Replay tutorial item: the shift lead's tips start again, and your plant and progress are untouched.

How do I start production?

Open the Production tab, buy blanks and add an order to the queue. The machines start on their own. If a machine breaks, tap it and call a repair: while it is down, the whole line waits. Sell finished parts on the Market tab at the current price or on a contract. If you are unsure what to do, read the shift lead's message at the bottom of the screen.

How do I unlock the second shop?

The Shops tab shows plant cards. The card for Assembly shop No. 2 lists what it takes to buy it: the required plant level, a site warehouse (bought on the Production tab) and the ERP IV technology (Tech tab), plus the price. Once every condition has a check mark, the Buy button appears.

How do I recover a save?

The game does not sync over the internet, so deleting the app deletes your progress. It comes back if you restore your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup made while the game was installed. If a slot says “This save can't be read”, the other slots are fine: open another one or start a new game in that slot.
