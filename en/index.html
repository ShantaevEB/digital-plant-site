Digital Plant is a factory simulator for iPhone. You take over a small machine shop that still runs by hand: a saw, a CNC machine, repairs on call, orders in a logbook. Step by step you turn it into a digital plant where machines, orders and purchasing are run by SCADA, MES and ERP.

You queue orders, buy blanks, fix machines, watch OEE (the share of useful time on your line), take contracts and sell parts on the market. The money goes into technologies from three vendors, a site warehouse, your own R&D institute and a second, assembly shop. Along the way come shift tasks, a plant chronicle and certificates on the honour board in the office.

The shop is alive: workers walk their shift, a crane carries blanks, the lights come on at dusk. Beyond the fence lies an industrial town with a bus, cars and a freight train. The game works offline, shows no ads and sells nothing inside: buy it once and play. Saves stay on your phone only. Russian, English and Spanish.
